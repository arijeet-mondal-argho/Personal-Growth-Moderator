# 🚀 Personal Growth Moderator

A complete personal growth platform that combines productivity, fitness, nutrition, study management, and intelligent coaching into a single application.

Built to help users improve their daily habits, achieve fitness goals, stay organized, and study more effectively—all from one dashboard.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📋 Task Management

* Create, edit, and delete tasks
* Schedule tasks with date and time
* Daily recurring tasks
* Task completion tracking
* Reminder support
* Progress monitoring

### 🍽️ Nutrition & Bodybuilding

* Meal logging
* Protein, carbohydrate, fat, and calorie tracking
* Custom meal plans
* Reusable diet templates
* Meal reminders
* Weight goal management
* Weight progress visualization

### 💪 Activity & Workout Tracking

* Workout logging
* Exercise history
* Calories burned tracking
* Daily activity summaries
* Weekly activity reports
* Fitness progress monitoring

### 📚 Study Planner

* Subject-wise study planning
* Daily study goals
* Study session scheduling
* Completion tracking
* Study hour analytics
* Consistency monitoring

### 🤖 Personal Growth Assistant

A multilingual rule-based coaching chatbot that understands:

* English
* বাংলা (Bangla)
* Banglish (Romanized Bangla)

The chatbot provides evidence-based guidance on:

#### Study & Learning

* Active Recall
* Retrieval Practice
* Spaced Repetition
* Interleaving
* Focus Management
* Exam Preparation
* Memory Improvement
* Productivity Systems

#### Nutrition & Fitness

* Healthy Eating
* Macronutrient Guidance
* Weight Management
* Workout Recommendations
* Recovery Strategies
* Hydration

#### Habits & Productivity

* Time Management
* Goal Achievement
* Consistency Building
* Routine Optimization
* Habit Formation

## 🎯 Project Vision

Most productivity apps solve only one problem.

This project combines:

* Planning
* Nutrition
* Exercise
* Learning
* Coaching

into one unified ecosystem focused on long-term self-improvement.

The goal is to create a digital personal growth companion that helps users become more disciplined, healthier, and more productive.

---

## 🔒 Privacy First

This application follows a local-first philosophy.

### User Data

* Stored locally on the device
* No mandatory account creation
* No tracking by default
* No unnecessary data collection

### Chatbot

* Uses rule-based responses
* Does not diagnose medical conditions
* Does not replace professional advice
* Includes safety guardrails for health-related questions
